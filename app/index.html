Venue camera coverage certificate

Before an event, which floor area can the camera network actually see? This is measured in simulation by casting a ray from every camera to a 1 m grid at torso height and testing whether venue geometry blocks it — not estimated from a floor plan.

Where the blind area is

Each square metre of floor, coloured by how many of the four cameras have an unobstructed line to it. Hover any cell for its exact count.

Why each camera loses its view

Every camera is aimed at the same point and has the same field of view, so the whole difference is geometry. Cells are split into what the camera sees, what venue structure blocks, and what falls outside the lens.

What is real here, and what is not

Not measured in this run — roadmap:

  • Crowd-density rehearsal (surge through gates) — agent model did not pass its own physics check in this build, so no density number is claimed.
  • Volumetric smoke (Omniverse Flow) degrading camera visibility.
  • Perception-model testing against the rendered frames.
