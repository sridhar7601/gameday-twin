Where the blind area is

Every square metre of floor, coloured by how many cameras have a clear line to it. Red is floor no camera can see. Hover any cell for its exact count.

Why each camera loses its view

All four cameras share the same field of view and aim point, so every difference is caused by the building. Each bar splits into what the camera sees, what structure blocks, and what falls outside the lens.
